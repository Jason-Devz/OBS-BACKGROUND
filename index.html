OBS Integration Guide

Three simple steps to load any background directly into your broadcasting setup.

STEP 01

Copy Template Link

Click the "Copy OBS URL" button on your desired template from the library above.

STEP 02

Add Browser Source

Open OBS Studio, create a new "Browser" source under your scene, and paste the URL.

STEP 03

Set Canvas Scale

Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080 (or match your canvas resolution).
